About Rextech Studios

Rextech Studios builds apps, games, AR/VR, and metaverse experiences. Learn about our mission, vision, and expert team.

Mission

To design and deliver innovative digital solutions that empower businesses and engage users.

We pursue this mission through cutting-edge technology, creative thinking, and a commitment to quality that runs through every project we touch.

Vision

To become a trusted leader delivering high-quality solutions and lasting client relationships.

We aim to make a positive, responsible impact on our customers, communities, and the future through integrity and dependability.

Our Story

Built with Passion, Driven by Innovation

Rextech Studios is a creative technology studio in India focused on building mobile apps, games, AR/VR, and metaverse solutions. In just 2+ years, we've successfully delivered 100+ projects for 25+ clients worldwide.

From our headquarters at Vismaya, AVGC Arena, Infopark Kerala — one of India's premier technology hubs — we work with startups and enterprises globally to turn ambitious ideas into polished digital products.
