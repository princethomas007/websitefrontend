FAQs

We've Got the Answers You're Looking For

Quick answers about our game, AR/VR, and app development services.

Do you provide international services?+
Yes! Rextech Studios serves clients globally from our base in Kerala, India and Australia. We have delivered projects for clients across India, Southeast Asia, Europe, and beyond.
Which technologies do you use for game development?+
We primarily use Unity, Unreal Engine, and C# for game development. For AR/VR, we work with ARCore, ARKit, and Meta/XR SDKs. For mobile apps, we use Flutter, React Native, and native iOS/Android development.
Can I enroll in Rextech Academy as a beginner?+
Absolutely. Rextech Academy is designed for both beginners and experienced developers. Our courses start from the fundamentals and progress to advanced topics, with hands-on projects throughout.
